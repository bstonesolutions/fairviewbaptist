Your first Sunday

Here's exactly how it goes

No guessing, no surprises. From Main Street to the last amen, here is what a Sunday looks like.

1

Pull in and park

We are at 2294 Main Street, right in Clay. Come at 10:00am for Sunday School, or arrive around 10:45 to get settled before the 11:00am worship service.

2

We'll meet you at the door

A friendly face is waiting to welcome you and point you the right way. You are never left to figure it out alone.

3

Get your kids settled

Sunday School has Bible classes for every age at 10:00am, and our active youth ministry gives young people a place to grow. We will walk you to the right class.

4

Find a seat

Sit wherever you like. There are no assigned seats, and no spotlight on the new face in the room.

5

Worship together

At 11:00 we sing the old hymns, pray, and hear preaching straight from the King James Bible.

6

Stay as long as you like

Afterward, hang around and say hello, ask a question, or head on out. And if you want more, come back at 6:00pm for the evening service.

Good to know

Common questions

The things first-time visitors usually want to ask.

What about my kids?

Sunday School has Bible classes for all ages at 10:00am, and our active youth ministry gives young people a place to grow in the Lord. Ask at the door and we will point you to the right class.

How do I get there?

We are at 2294 Main Street in Clay, WV 25043, right in town. If you need a ride, our van ministry will pick you up free of charge. Call 304-587-4709 and we will get you on the route.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. You will see folks in their Sunday best and folks in work clothes, and every one of them is glad you came.

When are your services?

Sunday School at 10:00am, morning worship at 11:00am, evening service at 6:00pm, and Wednesday prayer meeting and Bible study at 7:00pm.

What Bible version do you use?

We preach and teach from the King James Version (KJV).

What is the music like?

Traditional congregational singing. Old hymns and gospel songs the whole church sings together.

Will I be singled out or put on the spot?

No. Expect a handshake and a warm hello, not a spotlight. Come in, find a seat, and be part of the service.

Will I be asked to give money?

No. As our guest you are never expected or asked to give. When the offering plate comes by, you are welcome to simply let it pass.

Can I watch online before I come in person?

Absolutely. Catch a service on The Overlook first, then come see us whenever you are ready.

What if I have doubts, or I am not sure what I believe?

You are especially welcome. You do not have to have it all figured out to walk through our doors. Bring your questions and come as you are.

I have accessibility needs. Can you help?

Yes. Let us know when you plan your visit, or call 304-587-4709, and we will do our best to have everything ready for you.
