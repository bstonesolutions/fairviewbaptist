Welcome

Welcome home to Fairview.

We are a church family in the hills of Clay County that believes the Bible, loves people, and preaches Christ crucified, buried, and risen again. However you come and whatever you carry, you will find a warm welcome, honest preaching, and a place to belong.

First time?

Walking in somewhere new takes courage. Here's what to expect.

No pressure and no awkward spotlight. Just a warm mountain welcome from the moment you pull in off Main Street.

When & where

We are at 2294 Main Street in Clay. Come at 10:00am for Sunday School, or plan to arrive around 10:45 for the 11:00am worship service.

What to wear

Come in whatever you have. You will see folks in their Sunday best and folks in work clothes, and every one of them is glad you came.

Your kids

Sunday School has Bible classes for all ages at 10:00am, and our active youth ministry gives young people a place to grow in the Lord.

Need a ride?

Our van ministry would be glad to pick you up for church, free of charge. Call 304-587-4709 and we will save you a seat on the van too.

What we believe
"I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help."
Psalm 121:1 · KJV

An independent, fundamental Baptist church that stands on the King James Bible. We believe the good news that God saves sinners through the death, burial, and resurrection of Jesus Christ, and that this truth changes everything.

Ministries

Help for the hurting, hope for the whole county.

Fridays

H.O.P.E. Recovery

A Christ-centered addictions recovery program that meets Friday evenings. If you or someone you love is fighting addiction, there is help and hope here. No judgment, just the gospel and people who care.

Every week

Van Ministry

No way to get to church? Our van runs every service, free of charge, and our team goes out on Saturdays to visit. Call 304-587-4709 to get on the route.

Students

Youth Ministry

An active youth group where young people hear the Bible taught straight, build real friendships, and serve together.
