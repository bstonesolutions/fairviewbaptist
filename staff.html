Pastor Michael Spurlock

Pastor

Pastor Michael Spurlock began serving as pastor of Fairview Baptist Temple in the summer of 2024. With over eight years of full time ministry, his calling has been marked by a passion for souls, Bible centered preaching, and a heart to reach Clay County with the gospel of Jesus Christ. Before coming to Fairview he served at Mt. Pleasant Baptist Church in Elkview, WV and Hanes Baptist Church in Winston-Salem, NC. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Theology and a Master's in Biblical Exposition from Andersonville Theological Seminary.
