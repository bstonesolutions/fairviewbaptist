There is no special Sunday you need to wait for. Any Sunday is a good Sunday to visit Fairview Baptist Temple, and this is what you can expect when you do.

Sunday School starts at 10:00am with classes for all ages, and morning worship follows at 11:00am. We sing the old hymns, we pray, and we hear plain preaching from the King James Bible. In the evening we gather again at 6:00pm, and on Wednesday at 7:00pm we meet for prayer and Bible study. Services usually last about an hour, and nobody will put you on the spot.

Getting here

We meet at 2294 Main Street in Clay. If you need a ride, our van ministry will gladly come get you at no cost. Just call the church at 304-587-4709 and we will work out the details.

Come as you are. You do not need to dress a certain way or know anybody ahead of time. Bring your family, bring your questions, and give us a chance to make you feel at home.
