Welcome to the new online home of Fairview Baptist Temple. We are glad you found us, and we hope this site helps you get to know our church family here in Clay, West Virginia.

Take a few minutes and look around. You can find our service times, watch sermons on The Overlook, keep up with events, share a prayer request on the prayer wall, and give online. Whether you have been part of Fairview for years or you are just curious about us, everything here is meant to help you take the next step.

A website is a fine tool, but it is no substitute for being together. We would love to see you in person. Join us on Sunday for Sunday School at 10:00am, morning worship at 11:00am, and our evening service at 6:00pm, or come pray and study with us on Wednesday at 7:00pm. You will find us at 2294 Main Street in Clay, and Pastor Michael Spurlock and our whole church family will be glad to meet you.

The psalmist wrote, "I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help" (Psalm 121:1). That verse fits this place, and it fits our prayer for you. Wherever you are and whatever you are facing, our help comes from the Lord, and His door is open. So is ours.
