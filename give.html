Good to know

Questions about giving

Is online giving secure?

Yes. Online gifts are processed by Anedot, a trusted giving provider used by churches and ministries, over an encrypted connection. The church never sees or stores your card details.

Do I have to give online?

Not at all. You are welcome to place your gift in the offering at any service, or mail a check to Fairview Baptist Temple, PO Box 700, Clay, WV 25043.

Will I receive a giving record?

Yes. We can provide a record of your gifts for your files. For questions about the tax treatment of a gift, please consult a qualified tax professional.

Can I give to a specific fund?

Yes. Choose from the funds shown in the giving form above. The selected fund is included with your gift so we can direct it correctly.

How do I change or cancel a monthly gift?

Your emailed Anedot receipt includes a link to view your giving and manage a recurring gift. Or call the church at 304-587-4709 and we will be glad to help.

Need a hand?

If anything is not working or you have a question about giving, reach out or call 304-587-4709. We are glad to help.
